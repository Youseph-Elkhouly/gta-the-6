# Character demo

Satirical cameos in the loaded downtown. They are not the real people, and nothing here says they endorse the game. Every requested landmark had a street inside the city, so nobody was moved.

Search the name in the place box and choose Go there. You land on foot, facing them, about two metres away. Press E. Space, Enter, or a click advances. Esc closes.

`teleportToCharacter(id)` is on `window.CITY`.

A two-minute pass: Drake, Tobi Lütke, Sam Altman, Jensen Huang, Geoffrey Hinton.
